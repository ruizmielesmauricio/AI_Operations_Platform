## What the pilot is

Businesses taking part in the ORLA pilot use the whole product free of charge. You do not need to enter card or bank details. The pilot covers your shop, every branch you add during the pilot and your staff seats.

Pilot access is set up by the ORLA team for your business; it cannot be switched on from inside the app. If you think your shop should be on the pilot but it is not, contact ORLA support.

## How to tell if you are on the pilot

On Company Profile, next to your business name, the status shows "Free pilot until" and the date the pilot ends. The owner also sees the line "No payment needed during the pilot — branches and staff seats are free too." The buttons for adding a branch or an employee say "(free during pilot)".

You also get a notification in the Billing category when your pilot starts.

## Branches and staff during the pilot

- A branch you add during the pilot gets the same free access, up to the same end date. No payment step is shown.
- An employee you add during the pilot gets a free seat straight away. Their access starts as soon as they sign up with the invite link. See "Staff seats and invite links".

## When the pilot ends

The status on Company Profile changes to "Pilot ended" and the owner gets a Billing notification. Your data is kept: sales, stock, reports and settings are not deleted. Uploads and imports pause until the shop has an active subscription; the owner can select "Subscribe" on Company Profile to continue. See "Billing and your subscription".
