## When to contact support

Search the help articles first, or ask ORLA a how-to question in Ask ORLA. If the answer is not in the help articles, contact the ORLA team. You might also need support to change your business type, join the pilot, or ask for a copy or erasure of your data.

## How to contact support

There are two ways:

- In Ask ORLA: when ORLA cannot answer a how-to question from the help articles, it says so and shows a "Contact support" button under its reply. Select it to send your question.
- On the Help page: select "Contact support" at the top, type your question and select "Send to support".

You see a confirmation that your question was sent. You can send up to 10 support questions per business a day.

## What is sent

ORLA records your question, who asked it, which business it is about and the date and time. It does not attach your sales or stock data. Only send what the team needs to understand the problem, and never send passwords or card details.

The shop owner gets a Support notification for every question sent from their business, so they know what their team has asked.

## What happens next

The ORLA team reviews each question and follows up with you directly at your login email address. Replies are not instant: during the pilot every question is read and answered by a person.
