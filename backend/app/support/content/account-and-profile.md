## Change your login email

1. Open Account from the top menu.
2. Under "Change login email" enter the new email twice and your current password, then select "Change email".
3. Open the confirmation link sent to your new address. Your login email does not change until you click it, and depending on your account's security settings you may also need to confirm from your old address.

If you sign in with Google there is no password step. Only shop owners can change their login email; employees should ask their shop owner.

## Change your password

On the Account page select "Reset your password", or use "Forgot password?" on the log in page. See "Sign up and log in".

## Edit your company profile

1. Open Company Profile and select "View profile" next to the shop.
2. Edit the manager or owner name, contact email and phone, location label, address, city, postal code, country and timezone.
3. Save your changes.

Start typing the address and pick a suggestion to fill it in. Only the owner can edit the company profile.

## Add your logo

On the shop's profile page, under "Company logo", select "Upload logo" (or "Replace logo") and choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image. It appears in the header on every page. Select "Remove logo" to take it off. Branches use the main shop's logo unless they have their own. Only the owner can change the logo.

## Delete a shop

On Company Profile select "Delete this shop" and confirm with "Yes, delete". This cancels the shop's subscription and archives it. Your sales, products and reports stay on record, but you lose access to them in ORLA. Each account can have one standalone shop, so delete it first if you want to create a different one.

## Exporting or erasing your data

A self-service data export and full account erasure are not available in the app yet. To ask for a copy of your data or for it to be erased, contact ORLA support and the team will handle it for you.
