## What a branch is

Each physical location is its own branch in ORLA, with its own uploads, dashboard, reports and stock. Your account has one standalone shop; every extra location is added as a branch of it. Combining two locations' data in one shop is not supported. ORLA warns you if an upload looks like it has more than one location in it.

## Add a branch

1. Open Company Profile. Only the owner can add branches.
2. Next to your shop select "+ Add a branch". During a free pilot it says "(free during pilot)"; otherwise a branch costs €30 a month.
3. Fill in the "Branch profile": the branch name, manager or owner name, contact email and phone, a location label such as "Dublin - Rathmines", the address, city, postal code, country and timezone. Every field is required.
4. Select "Add branch" during a pilot, or "Save and continue to payment" otherwise and complete the payment.

Branches added during a pilot get the same free access, up to the same end date. If you cancel the form after the branch was created, ORLA asks "Discard this new branch?" and archives it.

## Switch between branches

Most pages have a "Business" or "Shop" drop-down when you have more than one location. Branches are shown with an arrow (↳) under the main shop. On Company Profile, "Business context for navigation" chooses which branch the top menu links open.

## See all branches together

- Dashboard: tick "Combine all branches into one view".
- Ask ORLA: choose "All branches" in the Business drop-down, or name a branch in your question, such as "What was revenue in Galway last week?".

Branches can only be combined when they share the same timezone. Weather-based rankings and the revenue-by-category chart are shown one branch at a time.

## Remove a branch

On Company Profile, select "Delete this shop" on the branch and confirm with "Yes, delete". This cancels the branch's subscription and archives it. Its sales, products and reports stay on record but you lose access to them in ORLA.
