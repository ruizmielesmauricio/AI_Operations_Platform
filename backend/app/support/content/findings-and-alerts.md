## What findings are

Findings are issues ORLA spots in your data using fixed rules, each with a plain-language recommendation and the figures that triggered it. They appear in the Findings & Recommendations section of the Dashboard, in the Action Plan of every report, and on the Welcome page under "Newest findings". They are split into "Business performance" and "Stock & products".

The same data always gives the same findings. ORLA's AI never decides what is flagged.

## What ORLA checks for

- Revenue decline: revenue fell by 10% or more against the previous period.
- Low gross margin: overall gross margin is below 20%.
- Incomplete cost data: less than half of revenue has a recorded cost price, so margin figures are unreliable.
- High return rate: returns are 10% or more of sales.
- Product selling at a loss: a product's cost is higher than its selling price.
- High revenue, thin margin: one of your top sellers has a margin well below your shop's overall margin.
- Low stock: a product has fallen below its reorder point.
- Negative stock: recorded stock went below zero, usually because sales were imported without a matching stock count or purchases.
- Dead stock: products with stock on hand and no sales in the period.
- Weather patterns: where your own sales history shows a link between the weather and sales of a category, and the coming week's forecast matches it.

## Acting on a recommendation

Each recommendation names what to look at, for example reviewing a price, reordering a product or running a promotion on dead stock. Use the drill-down links on the Dashboard to see the transactions behind it. Once the data changes (for example after you import new stock), the finding updates or disappears on its own.

## Active alerts

The Active Alerts section lists products whose stock is below their reorder point right now. Alerts update every time stock data changes, such as after a sales, stock count or purchases import, so you do not have to wait for a report. You also get a Stock notification summarising how many products are low.

To change when a product counts as low, set its reorder point on the Reorder rules page. See "Reorder rules and low-stock alerts".

## Nothing to flag

"Nothing to flag for this period." means none of the rules above were triggered for the selected dates. If you have not uploaded data yet, the Welcome page says "No findings yet" with a link to upload.
