## What ORLA costs

- Your shop: €80 a month.
- Each additional branch: €30 a month, billed as its own subscription.
- Each employee seat: €5 a month, up to 2 per business.

VAT is added at checkout where it applies. Businesses on the free pilot pay nothing for the shop, branches or seats. See "Free pilot access".

## Subscribe

1. Open Company Profile. Only the owner can manage billing.
2. Select "Subscribe" next to your shop (for a branch the button says "Complete payment (€30/mo)").
3. You are taken to Stripe's secure checkout page to enter your payment details. ORLA never sees or stores your card or bank details.
4. When payment is confirmed, the status changes to "Subscribed". This can take a few seconds.

## Manage billing, update your card or cancel

Select "Manage billing" next to the shop on Company Profile. This opens Stripe's customer portal, where you can update your payment method, see your invoices and cancel the subscription.

## Subscription statuses

On Company Profile, each shop shows one of:

- Subscribed: paid and active.
- Free pilot until (date): on the free pilot.
- Pilot ended: the pilot is over and there is no paid subscription yet.
- Pending Payment: no active subscription yet.
- Cancelled: the subscription was cancelled.

## If there is no active subscription

Your data is never deleted because a subscription lapses. You can still log in and see your existing dashboard and reports, but uploads and imports pause, and the Upload data link in the menu shows "(subscribe first)". Subscribing again turns them back on.

## A payment failed

Stripe retries failed payments. Use "Manage billing" to update your card or bank details. For an employee seat, the Team list shows "Payment failed" and you get a Team notification.
