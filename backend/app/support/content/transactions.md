## The Transactions page

Open Transactions from the top menu to see the individual records ORLA has imported, newest first, in tabs:

- Sales: date, product, quantity, unit price, total and order reference.
- Purchases: date, product, quantity, unit cost, supplier and PO or reference.
- Repairs: completed date, description, price charged, labour cost and job or reference. Only for business types that include repairs.

Use "From" and "To" to limit the dates. Lists show 25 records per page with buttons to move between pages. If you have branches, pick one under "Shop".

"Unmatched product" means the row could not be linked to a product, usually because the file had neither a product name nor a SKU for that line.

## Coming from the dashboard

Drill-down links on the Dashboard open this page already filtered, for example to one product or category. The page says "Filtered from a dashboard drill-down — reload this page directly to clear the filter." Open Transactions from the top menu to see everything again.

## Search

The search box under the top menu searches the business you are looking at for products, sales, purchases, suppliers and repairs. Type at least 2 characters, such as a product name, SKU, order or PO number, or supplier name, and choose a result to open it. Search only looks inside the selected shop or branch, never your other branches.

## Fixing a wrong record

You cannot edit an individual transaction in ORLA. To fix wrong data, correct the file, then undo the import and upload it again. See "Undo an import and re-import a file".
