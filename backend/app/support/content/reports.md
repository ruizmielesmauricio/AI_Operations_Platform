## When reports are created

ORLA creates two kinds of report automatically:

- Weekly: every Monday at 08:00 in your business timezone, covering the previous full week (Monday to Sunday).
- Monthly: on the 1st of each month at 08:00 in your business timezone, covering the previous calendar month.

When both fall on the same day you get two separate reports. Each new report also creates a Reports notification. Reports are shown in ORLA only; they are never emailed.

## Open a report

Open Reports from the top menu. The table lists each report's type, period, when it was generated and the date it is "Available until". Select a report to open it. Use the section links at the top to jump around.

## What a report contains

- Executive Summary: revenue, transactions, average sale, gross margin, inventory value, low-stock and dead-stock counts and the top recommendations.
- Revenue Performance: revenue against the previous week or month, returns and gross profit by product.
- Sales Performance: top sellers by units and by revenue.
- Category Breakdown: revenue, expenses and stock value per category.
- Inventory Health: inventory value, sell-through, turnover, stock cover, fast movers, slow movers and dead stock.
- Forecast & Future Outlook and Purchasing Recommendations.
- Workshop Performance, for business types that include repairs.
- Action Plan: the findings and recommendations for the period.

Sections that do not apply, or have too little data, say so instead of showing a misleading figure. Every number comes from the same fixed calculations as the dashboard; no AI is used to write a report.

## Generate reports now

You do not have to wait for Monday or the 1st. An owner or manager can select "Generate reports now" (or "Refresh reports with latest data" once reports exist) on the Reports page. ORLA builds last week's and last month's reports from the data uploaded so far, replacing the current ones for those periods. Staff cannot use this button.

## Download as PDF or Word

Open a report and select "Download PDF" or "Download DOCX" at the top right. The file is only created when you ask for it.

## Why a report disappeared (7-day availability)

Each report stays available for 7 days after it is generated; the "Available until" date shows exactly when. After that it is removed from the list. Download a PDF or Word copy if you want to keep it, or use "Generate reports now" to rebuild the latest week and month.

## No reports yet

New businesses see "No reports yet." Reports appear after your first completed week or month with data, or straight away if an owner or manager selects "Generate reports now". Upload your sales first so there is something to report on.
