## Where suppliers come from

Suppliers are created automatically when you import a purchases file with a supplier column, or confirm a supplier invoice PDF. You can also add them by hand. Purchases without a supplier are shown as "Unknown", which is fine: you can correct or merge suppliers at any time.

## Add a supplier

1. Open Suppliers from the top menu.
2. Under "Add a supplier" type the "Name" and, if you like, "Contact info (optional)".
3. Select "Add supplier".

## Edit, merge or deactivate

In the Suppliers table:

- Edit: change a supplier's name or contact details.
- Merge: if the same supplier appears twice (for example "Parts Hub" and "Parts Hub Ireland Ltd"), choose the one to keep under "Merge into…" and select "Confirm merge". Purchase history moves to the supplier you keep. Only the owner can merge suppliers.
- Deactivate: the supplier is no longer offered when matching new purchases. Existing purchase history and spend figures are kept.

Owners and managers can add, edit and deactivate suppliers. Staff can view the page.

## Spend by supplier

"Spend by supplier (last 30 days)" shows, for each supplier, the total spend on purchases with a known cost, the number of products and the number of purchases. If part of your spend has no supplier recorded, ORLA tells you the percentage; map a supplier column on future purchases uploads to close the gap.

## Lead times

A supplier's typical delivery time (lead time) is used to recommend reorder points. Lead times cannot be entered on screen yet, so reorder recommendations use the general default for now. See "Reorder rules and low-stock alerts".
