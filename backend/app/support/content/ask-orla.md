## What you can ask

Open Ask ORLA from the top menu and type a question. ORLA answers two kinds of question:

About your business data, for example:

- "How is my revenue doing?" or "What was revenue on the 24th of June?"
- "What are my top sellers?" or "What's my biggest expense?"
- "What should I reorder for the next two weeks?"
- "How much stock of Chain Lube do I have?" or "What did I order under PO-123?"
- "How did the workshop do last month?" (business types with repairs)
- "Does the weather affect sales of accessories?" or "Will the weather affect sales this week?"
- "What does sell-through mean?"
- "Summarise my latest report."

About using ORLA, for example "How do I upload my stock count?" or "Why did my report disappear?". These are answered from the ORLA help articles, with a link to the article. See "Contact ORLA support" if the answer is not there.

You can ask up to three things in one message, such as "What's my revenue and what should I reorder?".

## How ORLA stays accurate

ORLA is not a general chatbot. Every number it gives comes from the same fixed calculations as your dashboard, and every how-to answer comes from the help articles. A check runs on every answer before you see it; if an answer contains a figure that is not in your data, ORLA shows "I couldn't confidently answer that from your actual data" instead. Questions outside these topics get a plain reply listing what ORLA can help with, rather than a guess.

## Follow-up questions

ORLA remembers your last question and answer only, so a follow-up such as "And last month?" or "What about the previous period?" works. It does not remember anything further back. Changing the business in the drop-down starts afresh.

## Branches

If you have branches, choose one in the "Business" drop-down, choose "All branches" to combine them, or just name a branch in your question.

## Limits and availability

Each business can ask up to 200 questions a day; after that ORLA says "This business has reached its question limit with ORLA for today." If the AI service is down, ORLA says it is temporarily unavailable. Your dashboard and reports keep working either way.
