## Add an employee

1. Open Company Profile. Only the owner can add employees.
2. Next to the shop select "+ Add employee". During a free pilot it says "(free during pilot)"; otherwise a seat costs €5 a month.
3. Fill in their first name, surname, email address and role (Staff or Manager). The address is optional. Use the employee's own email address; it cannot already belong to someone on this shop.
4. Select "Add employee" during a pilot, or "Save and continue to payment" otherwise.

Each business can have up to 2 employee seats.

## Send the sign-up link

The employee needs to create their own ORLA account with the email address you entered.

- ORLA shows the sign-up link straight away with a "Copy link" button. Send it to the employee by text, WhatsApp or email.
- In the Team list you can also select "Copy invite link" or "Send invitation" at any time until they have signed up.

The link opens the sign-up page with their email already filled in. When they sign up with that address, ORLA links their account to your shop automatically.

## Seat status

In the Team list, each employee shows a status:

- "Active": they have signed up and can use ORLA.
- "Free pilot seat — waiting for them to sign in" or "Paid — waiting for them to sign in": the seat is ready; they have not signed up yet.
- "Pending payment" or "Pending payment and signup": the seat still needs to be paid for.
- "Payment failed": the seat's payment did not go through.
- "Removed": the employee was removed.

Access starts once the person has signed up and the seat is active (paid, or free during the pilot).

## What each role can do

- Owner: everything, including Company Profile, the logo, branches, employees, billing, deleting a shop, merging suppliers and changing their login email.
- Manager: everything a staff member can do, plus "Generate reports now", changing reorder points, and adding, editing or deactivating suppliers.
- Staff: view the dashboard, reports, Reorder rules, suppliers, transactions and notifications, upload and import data, and use Ask ORLA.

Managers and staff do not see the Team, Billing, Branches, Security & Account or Support notification categories.

Employees can update their own name and address with "Edit my profile" on Company Profile.

## Edit or remove an employee

In the Team list select "Edit" to change their name, role or address. The email address cannot be changed, because it is how ORLA matches them to their account.

Select "Delete" to remove an employee. Confirm with "Remove employee". This removes their access straight away and cancels their seat; they stay in the list as Removed for your records.
