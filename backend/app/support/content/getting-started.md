## What ORLA does

ORLA turns the exports you already have from your till, POS or spreadsheets into a dashboard, weekly and monthly reports, a demand forecast and restock suggestions. You do not need to reformat your files: you upload them as they are and ORLA works out what each column means.

Every number ORLA shows is calculated by fixed rules from your own data. The Ask ORLA assistant only explains those numbers in plain language; it never makes up a figure.

## Your first five steps

1. Create your account on the sign-up page with your email address and a password, or with Google. See "Sign up and log in".
2. Create your business on the Company Profile page: give it a name, pick your business type and check the timezone. See "Choose your business type".
3. Go to Upload data and upload your sales export. For the best results start with the last 12 months of sales, then add your current stock count and recent purchases. See "Uploading your data".
4. Confirm what each column means when ORLA asks, then select "Run import".
5. Open the Dashboard to see your revenue, margin, stock and forecast, or ask ORLA a question such as "How is my revenue doing?".

## Where to find things

The top menu has a link to every part of ORLA: Dashboard, Upload data, Reports, Ask ORLA, Reorder rules, Suppliers, Transactions, Notifications, Company Profile, Account and Help. The search box under the menu finds products, sales, purchases, suppliers and repairs in the business you are looking at.

When you log in you land on the Welcome page, which shows your newest findings. Select "Go to your Dashboard" for the full picture. If you have not created a business yet, ORLA takes you to Company Profile instead.

## Getting help

Search these help articles from the Help page, or ask ORLA a how-to question in Ask ORLA, for example "How do I upload my stock count?". If the answer is not in the help articles, ORLA offers a "Contact support" button that sends your question to the ORLA team. See "Contact ORLA support".
