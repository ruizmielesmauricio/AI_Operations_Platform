## The sections

The Dashboard has these sections, listed under "On this page" so you can jump to one:

- Financial Performance: revenue (with the change against the previous period), returns, gross margin, a revenue-by-category chart and a gross profit by product chart.
- Retail Operations: inventory value, sell-through rate, top sellers (most sold and most revenue), stock cover and dead stock.
- Workshop Performance: repairs completed, revenue, average ticket and gross margin (labour only). Only for business types that include repairs.
- Forecast: expected revenue for the next 7, 14 or 30 days, and "Products to watch" with a suggested reorder. See "The forecast".
- Findings & Recommendations: issues ORLA found, each with a recommendation. See "Findings, recommendations and alerts".
- Active Alerts: products that are low on stock right now.

Hover over a metric's name to see a short definition. See "What each metric means" for all of them.

## Change the date range

Use "From" and "to" at the top to choose the period. By default the dashboard shows the last 30 days; select "Reset to default (last 30 days)" to go back. Changes against the previous period compare with the period of the same length just before.

## Filter by category

Financial Performance, Retail Operations, Forecast and Findings each have a "Category" filter. Pick a category to see only its products, or "All categories". Categories come from the category column in your uploads; if you have none yet, import a file with a category column.

## Branches

If you have branches, pick one under "Business". Tick "Combine all branches into one view" to add every branch together. The revenue-by-category chart is shown per branch only.

## Drill down to the transactions

Every figure comes from your own transactions. Use links such as "View individual sale transactions →", "View transactions →" next to a top seller, or "View individual repairs →" to open the Transactions page filtered to those records. See "Transactions and search".

## If the dashboard is empty

Sections show messages such as "No sales in this period." or "Not enough recent sales to estimate stock cover." when there is not enough data for the selected dates. Check that you have imported sales for that period on Upload data, or widen the date range.

## Margin shows as an estimate

Gross margin needs cost prices. If most sales in the period have no recorded cost, the margin is marked as an estimate and products with no cost price are left out of the margin ranking. Upload a stock count or purchases file with a unit cost column to fill the gap.
