## Create your business

1. Open Company Profile from the top menu. The first time you log in, ORLA takes you there automatically.
2. Under "Create a business", type your business name.
3. Pick your "Business type" from the list. A short description under the list shows what that type includes.
4. Check the "Timezone". It starts as Europe/Dublin, which is right for businesses in Ireland. Change it if your shop is somewhere else (for example Europe/London).
5. Select "Create business".

Each account can have one standalone shop. To add another location, add it as a branch of that shop instead. See "Branches".

## The 19 business types

Retail:

- Bike shop (includes repairs and servicing)
- General retail / gift shop
- Clothing, shoes & accessories
- Electronics & phone shop (includes repairs and servicing)
- Outdoor & sports shop (includes repairs and servicing)
- Hardware & DIY
- Books & stationery
- Toys & games
- Pet shop
- Garden centre
- Convenience store & grocery
- Jewellery & watches (includes repairs and servicing)
- Other shop with a repair or service workshop (includes repairs and servicing)

Food & drink:

- Café / coffee shop
- Restaurant
- Bakery
- Bar / pub
- Takeaway / fast food
- Deli / speciality food shop

Every type gets the same core: sales, stock, purchases, suppliers, the dashboard, reports, the forecast, findings and Ask ORLA. The types marked "includes repairs and servicing" also get a Repairs upload type, the Workshop Performance section on the dashboard and in reports, and a Repairs tab in Transactions.

## Why your business type matters

The business type decides which extra modules you see (for example repairs) and helps ORLA word its answers for your kind of business. It never changes how a number is calculated.

## Why the timezone matters

ORLA stores times in UTC but works out days, weeks and months in your business timezone. Weekly reports run on Monday at 08:00 and monthly reports on the 1st of the month at 08:00 in that timezone, and "today" in the dashboard and in Ask ORLA means today where your shop is. The owner can change the timezone later from the shop's profile: open Company Profile and select "View profile".

## Changing your business type

The business type is chosen when the business is created and is not shown as an editable field on the profile page. If you picked the wrong type, contact ORLA support before uploading data.
