## Create your account

1. Open the sign-up page and enter your email address.
2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and type it again in "Retype password". Tick "Show passwords" if you want to check what you typed.
3. Select "Sign up".
4. ORLA sends a confirmation link to your email address. Open the email and click the link to finish creating your account. If it has not arrived, check your spam folder or select "Resend confirmation email".

You can also select "Continue with Google" to sign up with your Google account instead of a password.

After your account is ready, ORLA takes you to Company Profile so you can create your business.

## If you were invited by your employer

If a shop owner added you as an employee, they will send you a sign-up link, by email or by copying it to you directly. The link opens the sign-up page with your email address already filled in. Use that same email address: it is how ORLA connects your new account to the shop. See "Staff seats and invite links".

## Log in

Open the log in page, enter your email and password and select "Log in", or select "Continue with Google" if you signed up with Google. You arrive on the Welcome page.

## Forgot your password

1. On the log in page select "Forgot password?".
2. Enter your email address and select "Send reset link".
3. If an account exists for that address, ORLA emails you a link to reset the password. Follow the link, choose a new password and select "Save new password".

When you reset your password, ORLA signs out your other sessions, so anyone still logged in on another device has to log in again with the new password.

If you signed up with Google, you do not have an ORLA password; log in with Google instead.

## Log out

Select "Log out" at the right of the top menu on any page.
