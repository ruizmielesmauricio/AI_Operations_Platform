## The Notification Centre

Open Notifications from the top menu. The number in brackets is how many notifications you have not read yet. Each notification has a title, a short explanation, a category tag and, where it helps, a button that takes you straight to the next step, for example to Reorder rules or a new report.

Notifications are shown in ORLA only. ORLA does not send them by email.

## Categories

- Stock: products below their reorder point.
- Data & Uploads: finished imports, failed imports, and reminders when your data is getting out of date.
- Reports: a new weekly or monthly report is ready, or a report is late or failed.
- ORLA Insights: notable findings, and a notice if the Ask ORLA assistant is temporarily unavailable.
- Team: employees added, activated, changed or removed.
- Billing: subscription changes, and the start and end of free pilot access.
- Branches: branch subscription changes.
- Security & Account: changes to your account security.
- Support: questions your team sent to ORLA support from Ask ORLA.

Team, Billing, Branches, Security & Account and Support notifications are only shown to the shop owner.

## Filters

Pick a category on the left, and use the filters at the top for severity, read status (Unread only, Read only, Dismissed) and date (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days or a Custom range). Up to 25 notifications are shown per page.

## Mark as read and dismiss

- Select "Mark read" on a notification, or "Mark all read" at the top.
- Select "Dismiss" to clear a notification you have dealt with. It moves to Dismissed. If the same issue comes back later, ORLA creates a new notification.

Some notifications update themselves instead of repeating: for example, the low-stock notification is updated with the new count each time stock changes.

## The status banner

If something is blocking normal use, such as a failed or late report, a failed import or the assistant being unavailable, a banner appears at the top of every page with a "View details" link. It disappears once the notification is dismissed.
