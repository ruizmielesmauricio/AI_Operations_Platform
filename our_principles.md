# Executive Summary (TL;DR)

This document defines the principles, values, and decision-making philosophy of the AI Operations Platform.

Every business, product, engineering, and architectural decision should align with these principles.

If a future decision conflicts with this document, either the decision must change, or this document must be deliberately amended.

This document is the company's constitution.

# Our Mission

To empower small and medium-sized businesses with enterprise-grade operational intelligence through Business Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

We exist to help business owners make better decisions—not simply provide more data.

---

# Our Vision

To become the leading AI Operations Platform for independent businesses by transforming business data into clear, trustworthy, and actionable recommendations.

Bike shops are our first vertical.

The platform is designed to expand into additional industries while maintaining the same core architecture.

---

# Our Values

## 1. Business First

We solve business problems before technology problems.

Technology is only valuable if it improves business outcomes.

---

## 2. Decisions Over Dashboards

Dashboards show information.

We build systems that recommend actions.

Every feature should help customers decide what to do next.

---

## 3. Business Logic First

Deterministic business logic always comes before Artificial Intelligence.

Calculations, forecasting models, SQL queries, and machine learning generate recommendations.

AI communicates those recommendations.

---

## 4. Explainability

Every recommendation must be understandable.

Customers deserve to know:

* What happened
* Why it happened
* How we reached the recommendation
* What action is suggested

No black-box recommendations.

---

## 5. AI Is a Tool, Not the Product

Artificial Intelligence enhances our platform.

It is not our product.

The product is operational decision support.

---

## 6. AI-Agnostic by Design

The platform must never depend on a single AI provider.

Every AI service should be replaceable without changing the core architecture.

This ensures:

* Lower operating costs
* Better flexibility
* Faster innovation
* Reduced vendor lock-in

---

## 7. Customer Data Is Sacred

Customer trust is our greatest asset.

Every system will be designed with:

* Security
* Privacy
* GDPR compliance
* Least-privilege access
* Data isolation between customers

Security is a design requirement, not an afterthought.

---

## 8. Build Once. Scale Everywhere.

Every architectural decision should support future expansion.

The platform should be reusable across industries while allowing each vertical to implement its own business rules.

---

## 9. Simplicity Wins

Small businesses should not require technical knowledge to use our platform.

If users need extensive training, we have failed.

Complexity belongs inside the software—not in the user experience.

---

## 10. Continuous Improvement

Every recommendation, model, workflow, and feature should evolve using customer feedback and measurable business outcomes.

The platform should improve with every release.

---

# Things We Will Never Do

We will never build features simply because they are fashionable.

We will never recommend actions we cannot explain.

We will never lock customers into a single AI provider.

We will never collect unnecessary customer data.

We will never prioritise impressive demonstrations over measurable customer value.

We will never sacrifice long-term architecture for short-term convenience without documenting the trade-off.

---

# Decision Framework

Every major decision should be evaluated from three perspectives.

## Business Perspective

Does this solve a meaningful customer problem?

Does it create measurable value?

---

## Engineering Perspective

Is it scalable?

Is it secure?

Is it maintainable?

Can it evolve over time?

---

## Commercial Perspective

Does it strengthen our competitive advantage?

Does it improve profitability?

Does it reduce operating costs?

Will customers pay for it?

---

# Decision Classification

Every important decision will be recorded using one of the following categories.

| Type                            | Description                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Business Decision (BD)**      | Pricing, customers, positioning, market strategy            |
| **Architecture Decision (ADR)** | Technology selection, infrastructure, software architecture |
| **Product Decision (PD)**       | Features, UX, workflows, product capabilities               |
| **Engineering Decision (ED)**   | Development standards, testing, deployment, code quality    |

---

# Feature Acceptance Rule

A feature should only be implemented if it improves at least one of the following:

* Saves customers time.
* Saves customers money.
* Reduces operational risk.
* Improves business decision-making.

If it achieves none of these objectives, it should not be built.
