## Common Questions

**"I'm not technical."**
Good — neither is the product. No SQL, no chart-building, no data science vocabulary. If you can email a spreadsheet, you can use this.

**"Can't I just ask ChatGPT?"**
You can, and it'll give you an answer that sounds confident. It won't be connected to your actual stock levels, it won't remember last quarter, and it can invent numbers. Ours calculates from your real data, every time.

**"My POS already has reports."**
It does — and they tell you *what* happened. They don't tell you why margin fell, which products to stop ordering, or what next month looks like. That's the gap.

**"What if my data is messy?"**
Most shops' data is. We validate it, tell you in plain language what's wrong, and show you what we can and can't conclude from it. We'd rather say "we can't tell you that yet" than make something up.
